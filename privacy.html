Privacy

Last updated 16 September 2026

APEXBOUTS publishes short animal-matchup videos. This page explains what happens to data in connection with that.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no cookies set by this site. It is a set of links and this page.

The publishing tool

Videos are uploaded to our own social accounts by a private tool that only we use. It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.

To upload, the tool is authorised against accounts we own on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. That authorisation lets it do one thing: upload videos to those accounts. The resulting access tokens are stored on a private computer and are never shared, sold or transmitted anywhere else.

The tool reads no personal data, no viewer information and no analytics. It does not access any account other than ours.

Google user data

Authorisation uses the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope only. That scope permits uploading videos to our own channel and nothing more. No Google user data is read, stored, shared or used for any other purpose. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Videos and viewers

Watching our videos happens on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook. Those platforms collect data under their own privacy policies, not ours. We see only the aggregate statistics each platform shows any account holder.

AI-generated content

Every video is generated with AI tools and labelled as such on each platform. No real animals are filmed and no footage is taken from anyone else.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us through any of the accounts linked on the home page.
